
# TrendPulse — Part 2: NumPy and Pandas

## 1. Project Overview

TrendPulse analyzes YouTube trending video statistics from the
United States, Great Britain, and India.

Part 2 focuses on NumPy numerical operations and Pandas data
cleaning, transformation, and analysis.

## 2. Tools and Technologies

- Python
- NumPy
- Pandas
- Google Colab
- Jupyter Notebook

## 3. Dataset

- Countries: US, GB, IN
- Total records after cleaning: 46,000
- Total columns: 24
- Categories: 14

## 4. Tasks Completed

1. Loaded datasets from three countries.
2. Created and analyzed NumPy arrays.
3. Performed indexing, slicing, sorting, and reshaping.
4. Applied vectorized numerical operations.
5. Combined datasets using Pandas.
6. Handled missing values and duplicate records.
7. Converted date and numeric columns.
8. Merged video records with category information.
9. Filtered and sorted video records.
10. Performed category-wise and country-wise aggregation.
11. Analyzed publishing days and hours.
12. Calculated days to trend and trending lifespan.
13. Generated channel-level summary reports.
14. Calculated engagement scores and identified the top 10%.

## 5. Key Findings

- Total cleaned records: 46,000.
- Entertainment was the largest category with 12,270 records.
- Music was the second-largest category with 10,310 records.
- Great Britain had the highest average views at approximately 1.58 million.
- India had approximately 952,101 average views.
- The United States had approximately 1.01 million average views.
- Average trending lifespan was approximately 3.05 days.
- Top 10% engagement records: 4,600.
